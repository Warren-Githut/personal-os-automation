Subject: Proposal: Golden Hour Dinner (evening promo, all 3 stores)

Hi [Boss Name],

I want to propose a new evening promotion and share the numbers behind it.

**The idea**
We launch **Golden Hour Dinner** at all three stores, 17:00 to 21:00, Monday to Sunday. Customers pick one starter and one main for a fixed 199k. No discounts, no fine print. Loyalty members get double points on evening orders at no cost to us.

**Why now**
Our evening kitchen and floor staff are already on the clock. Adding dinner covers in that window means almost pure contribution, since labor is a sunk cost at that hour. We already tested a similar evening push at LU5 and covers rose 24 percent. The morning version did nothing, so we dropped it.

**The math (per week, all 3 stores)**

| Store | Covers now | Covers with promo | Extra covers | Extra revenue |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| LU3 | 112 | 134 | +22 | +4.8M |
| LU5 | 100 | 120 | +20 | +5.2M |
| LU7 | 212 | 254 | +42 | +12.3M |
| **Total** | **424** | **508** | **+84** | **+22.3M** |

After food cost, that is about **16.3M contribution per week**, or roughly **70M per month**.

**How we protect the margin**
The 199k price already covers most dishes at healthy margin. For a few premium items like salmon, we ask the guest to add a small top-up so the combo still hits 60 percent margin. This keeps the entry price low to pull people in, while no dish loses money. The floor staff suggest it, they never force it.

| Main item | Combo cost | GP at 199k | Action |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| Carbonara Pasta | 70.4k | 64.6% | PASS (no add-on) |
| Beef Kimchi Fried Rice | 78.8k | 60.4% | PASS (no add-on) |
| Crispy Chicken Burger | 81.7k | 58.9% | Add-on +5.3k |
| Squid Ink Pasta w/ Crab | 86.4k | 56.6% | Add-on +16.9k |
| Cheese Bomb Sandwich | 90.7k | 54.4% | Add-on +27.9k |
| Crispy Skin Salmon | 144.1k | 27.6% | Add-on +161.1k |

**What it costs us**
Menu board and POSM, about 1 to 2M total. No extra labor. No discount given away.

**Proposed timeline**

| Week | Step |
|:---|:---|
| W30 | Print POSM, brief floor staff, port loyalty double points |
| W31 | Launch at all 3 stores |
| W32 | Check food margin, trim menu if below 55 percent |

**My recommendation**
Approve for W31. The upside is real, the downside is tiny, and we already have proof from LU5. I will watch the margin closely in the first week.

Happy to adjust before we print.

Best,
Warren
